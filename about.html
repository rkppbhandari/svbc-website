About Us

Rooted in Sanatan Dharma, guided by Vaidik traditions, and dedicated to faith, culture, education, community, and seva.

Who We Are

A Spiritual Home for Our Community

Shree Vaidik Bhakti Center of Greater Cincinnati is a nonprofit religious, cultural, educational, and charitable organization serving the Greater Cincinnati community.

Our Center is dedicated to preserving and promoting Sanatan Dharma and Vaidik traditions while providing a welcoming environment where individuals and families can worship, learn, celebrate, serve, and grow together.

Through spiritual programs, religious education, cultural activities, festivals, youth engagement, and community service, we seek to strengthen our connection to Dharma while sharing its timeless values with future generations.

ॐ

Our Mission

Preserving Dharma. Serving Community.

Our mission is to preserve and promote Sanatan Dharma and Vaidik traditions through worship, spiritual education, cultural preservation, community engagement, and selfless service.

We strive to create opportunities for people of all generations to deepen their spiritual understanding, celebrate their heritage, build meaningful community connections, and practice seva.

✦

Our Vision

A Thriving Spiritual Community

We envision a vibrant and welcoming spiritual community where Sanatan Dharma and Vaidik traditions continue to flourish for generations to come.

Our vision is to provide a place where devotion, knowledge, culture, service, and community come together to inspire individuals and strengthen families.

Our Spiritual Foundation

Sanatan Dharma & Vaidik Tradition

Sanatan Dharma provides the spiritual foundation of our organization. Through Vaidik worship, prayer, sacred traditions, festivals, education, and community gatherings, we seek to preserve and share this living spiritual heritage.

Our programs encourage devotion, ethical living, knowledge, respect for tradition, service to others, and spiritual growth for individuals and families.

Together as One Community

Unity & Inclusiveness

Shree Vaidik Bhakti Center seeks to create a welcoming environment where individuals and families can participate in spiritual, cultural, educational, and community activities.

We value mutual respect, cooperation, unity, and peaceful relationships within our community and with the wider Greater Cincinnati community.
