Our Mission

Faith, Culture, Education & Seva

ॐ

Our mission is to preserve and promote Sanatan Dharma and Vaidik traditions while providing opportunities for spiritual growth, religious and cultural education, community service, and meaningful participation for individuals and families in the Greater Cincinnati community.

Through worship, education, cultural programs, celebrations, youth activities, and seva, the Center seeks to strengthen community connections while encouraging unity, inclusiveness, and respect.
